## New

* Gesture trainer: measures gestures against the limits that SandFly uses with your settings. Go to Settings > Help & feedback > Gesture trainer, choose a group, such as One finger, Edge menus or Back tap, and make each gesture 3 times. The result of each try is spoken. After the last try, the screen lists the measurements of each try, what to change, and a button for each setting that changes the limits. The gesture trainer needs Android 13 or later.
* Triple-tap and Quadruple-tap: one-finger taps anywhere on the screen. They are in Settings > Gestures > 1 finger, and have no command by default. While one of them has a command, a double tap runs after the time between one-finger taps has passed. They need Android 13 or later.
* Keyboard practice: SandFly says each key pressed and the command that it runs, and runs nothing. Keys do not reach apps. Go to Settings > Keyboard shortcuts > Keyboard practice or Settings > Help & feedback > Keyboard practice, or use Turn keyboard practice on or off, in Settings > Commands > SandFly. The keys are SandFly key + K in the VoiceOver keymap, SandFly key + 1 in the NVDA and JAWS keymaps, SandFly key + H in the Orca keymap, and SandFly key + Shift + K in the keyboard phone keymaps. To turn it off, press the keys again, Back or Escape. The Keyboard practice lesson of the keyboard tutorial opens from Contents in the keyboard tutorial.
* Hold a touch on the focused item: puts a touch down on the middle of the focused item and holds it, for buttons that act while a finger stays on them, such as the voice message button in Telegram. Hold the keys to hold the touch, or press and release them within half a second to keep the touch down until you press them again, Back or Escape. The command is in Settings > Commands > Activation. The default keys are SandFly key + V in the keyboard phone keymaps. It needs Android 11 or later.
* Send key: on a keyboard phone, the Send key, such as the green call key of the Zinwa Q25, presses the Send button in the message field of a messaging app, whether Enter sends messages is on or off. In messaging apps, it also presses a Send button outside the message field, such as the one for a voice message being recorded. Elsewhere, the key goes to the phone as before.
* Speak password characters and Spell the password field: with the SandFly keyboard, SandFly says each character typed, deleted or passed over in a password field instead of "bullet". Go to Settings > Typing > Physical keyboard > Speak password characters. It is off by default. Spell the password field, in Settings > Commands > Text editing, spells the text of the password field with the position of each character. The keys are SandFly key + Y in the keyboard phone keymaps. The characters are not kept for Repeat last spoken phrase, Spell last spoken phrase, Copy last spoken phrase or Speech history.
* Auto-correct and Word suggestions: for the word typed with the SandFly keyboard. Auto-correct replaces a misspelled word when you type Space, punctuation or Enter, and Backspace right after a correction undoes it. Word suggestions lists corrections and completions for the word being typed: Up Arrow and Down Arrow, or the trackpad of the Zinwa Q25, move through them, and Enter types one. Speak suggestion says the correction or the first suggestion Immediately, or after a pause of 0.25 to 3 seconds; the default is 1 second. With Enter types the spoken suggestion on, Enter types the spoken suggestion; it is off by default. Word list language has 12 languages, and Delete learned words deletes the words learned from your typing. Go to Settings > Typing > Physical keyboard. Auto-correct and Word suggestions are off by default. Next word suggestion, Previous word suggestion and Type word suggestion are in Settings > Commands > Text editing, and Word suggestions is also a reading control.
* Fast search: in browse mode, the key you choose with a letter or digit moves to the next item whose text starts with that letter or digit, and with Shift to the previous one. Go to Settings > Keyboard shortcuts > Fast search > Fast search key. It is off by default.
* Browse mode in the Keyboard phone single-key keymap: the SandFly key pressed twice turns browse mode on or off, or SandFly key + Q where Alt is the SandFly key. In browse mode, letters move by element as in the SandFly keymap, such as B to the next button and K to the next link. In the keyboard phone keymaps, a shortcut can work only in browse mode, with Only in browse mode in the shortcut dialog.
* BlackBerry mode: gestures, keys and sounds similar to those of the BlackBerry 10 Screen Reader. Go to Settings > Settings profiles > BlackBerry mode, or use Turn BlackBerry mode on or off, in Settings > Commands > SandFly. The first time, SandFly makes the BlackBerry mode settings profile, with BlackBerry gestures, the BlackBerry keymap on keyboard phones, the BlackBerry sound theme, and no usage hints or list positions. BlackBerry gestures is also a choice in Settings > Gestures > Gesture layout. Quick Tasks, in Settings > Commands > SandFly, changes the speech volume and rate with swipes for 3 seconds. Next by navigation setting, Previous by navigation setting, Next navigation setting and Previous navigation setting can be assigned to gestures.
* Mute key: on phones with a mute key, such as the Zinwa Q25, hold the mute key in a text field to dictate. Recording stops when you release the key. Mute key press sets the command for a press of the mute key elsewhere. Go to Settings > Volume keys > Hold mute key to dictate in text fields, which is on by default, and Settings > Volume keys > Mute key press.
* Start voice editing: records voice commands for the focused text field one after another, without a gesture for each command, such as "select all", "copy", or "type" and a text. Voice editing ends when you run the command again, say "stop editing", or move focus out of the field, or when the time limit passes. The command is in Settings > Commands > Text editing. To set the time limit, go to Settings > Voice commands > Voice editing time limit. The default is 10 minutes.
* Voice commands for text in a text field: move the cursor, select, copy, cut and delete by a number of characters, words, sentences, lines or paragraphs, such as "delete next five words" or "copy previous 2 sentences"; move to the start or end of a word, sentence, line, paragraph or the text, such as "end of line"; and select, copy, cut or delete a text in the field, such as "select crocodile". The phrases are in Settings > Help & feedback > User guide, Appendix C.
* Answer call and End call: End call declines the ringing call, or ends the call in progress. Both commands also act on the calls of apps such as WhatsApp, with the buttons of the call notification. They are in Settings > Commands > System actions. Ending the call in progress of an app needs notification access for SandFly.
* App tiles and System tiles: command categories that activate the quick settings tiles of apps and of the system. They are in Settings > Commands. A system tile that SandFly has a control for, such as Wi-Fi or Hotspot, runs that control. With Shizuku running, Add system tile reads the tiles from the system.
* Search commands: a search field at the top of Settings > Commands lists the commands whose names contain the words you type.
* Perform gesture, Run command and Add to SandFly menu: on the screen of each command in Settings > Commands. Perform gesture assigns the next gesture you make. Run command closes SandFly settings and runs the command in the app shown before. Add to SandFly menu adds the command at the end of the SandFly menu.
* Help in SandFly settings: Help, in the app bar of most settings screens, opens the section of the user guide about the screen.
* Actions available: the Actions available sound plays for an item with actions from the app beyond the standard ones, such as Reply on a chat message. To set when it plays, go to Settings > Sound and vibration > Actions available sound: Off, When the item gets focus, the default, or After the item is spoken. To have SandFly say "Actions available" after such items, go to Settings > Verbosity > Speak Actions available. It is off by default.
* Choose from folder: when you replace a sound of a sound theme, Choose from folder lists the sound files of a folder, and the file with accessibility focus plays. Go to Settings > Sound and vibration > Sound themes > Sounds, and choose a sound.
* With headphones, centred otherwise: a 3D audio choice. Control sounds play in 3D with headphones, and from the centre on other outputs. Go to Settings > Sound and vibration > Sound themes > 3D audio.
* Speech rate step: sets how much the speech rate changes at a time with the Speech rate sliders, the Speech rate reading control and the speech rate commands. Go to Settings > Speech > Speech rate step. The default is 10% of the rate.
* Play sample: speaks a sample sentence with the engine, voice, volume, rate, pitch and voice effects of a voice profile. Go to Settings > Speech > Voice profiles, and choose a profile.
* Speak during media playback: speaks the screen-off and unlock announcements while media plays. Go to Settings > Verbosity > Screen on and off > Speak during media playback. It is off by default.
* Color detection and Light level tone: Live recognition modes. Color detection names the colors in the centre of the camera view, such as "Dark blue" or "Red and white". Light level tone plays a continuous tone whose pitch rises with the brightness of the camera view. Go to Settings > Image and screen descriptions > Live recognition.
* AI service reading control: swipe up or down to switch between the AI services that are set up. Add it in Settings > Reading controls. AI service: next value and AI service: previous value are in Settings > Commands > Setting values.
* Cyrillic text recognition: text recognition reads Russian, Ukrainian, Serbian and other languages written in Cyrillic, with a model that you download. Go to Settings > Image and screen descriptions > Cyrillic text recognition. Recognition with these models is slower than for other scripts.
* View code, Edit code and New script from code: View code, on the page of a script, shows the main.lua file of the script. Edit code changes the code of an installed script, and keeps the switch, permissions, gestures, keys, settings and stored data of the script. Edit code is not shown for scripts made in the script builder. To install a script from code that you enter, go to Settings > Scripts > New script from code.
* Keep the keyboard open after sending and Return to the previous keyboard: Keep the keyboard open after sending keeps the braille keyboard open after Submit text presses the send button next to a message field, as in Telegram, WhatsApp and Messages. Return to the previous keyboard changes back to the keyboard used before the braille keyboard when you hide the braille keyboard or leave the text field. Go to Settings > Braille > Braille keyboard. Both are off by default.
* Braille tables: Indonesian, uncontracted and Tusing contracted, for the braille keyboard and braille displays. Unicode Braille and Unicode 8-dot Braille type each cell as a Unicode braille character, on the braille keyboard and braille displays. Go to Settings > Braille > Braille keyboard > Languages > Add a language.
* New commands: New line (Text editing), which types a new line in a text field of several lines or performs the action of a field of one line, also by voice with "new line", "new paragraph" or "press enter"; Next sentence and Previous sentence (Navigate by text).

## Changes

* Letter keys press keypad buttons: on the lock screen, a digit key opens the PIN pad and types the digit, as a letter key that carries a number does. Where no PIN is needed, such as while Extend Unlock keeps the phone unlocked or with a swipe lock, a letter or digit key on the lock screen unlocks the phone.
* Enter sends messages: works in blindGram, Telegram Beta, Nekogram, exteraGram, Cherrygram, Forkgram and Octogram, as in Telegram.
* Arrow key navigation: while arrow key navigation is on, and in the keyboard phone keymaps, moving to a text field reads the field without giving it input focus, and the arrow keys move on past it. The field is edited once you activate it or type into it. In the keyboard phone keymaps, Enter activates such a field.
* SandFly keyboard: Shift pressed and released alone types the next key with Shift, as on BlackBerry keyboards. Shift pressed and released again cancels it.
* NVDA keymap: in browse mode, P and Shift + P move to the next and previous paragraph. They are Navigate to next paragraph, second key and Navigate to previous paragraph, second key in Settings > Keyboard shortcuts. Insert + F9 starts or ends selection mode.
* Reading controls that change a value: Adjust slider, Adjust value, Speech rate and the other reading controls that change a value follow the direction of the swipe. Swipe up or right increases the value, and swipe down or left decreases it.
* Activate: moves the cursor in a text field as a double tap does, from any gesture, voice command, braille command or script.
* Timing settings: Settings > Touch > Focus delay and Settings > Gestures > Gesture start time go up to 2 seconds. Settings > Gestures > Time between two-stroke swipes has 0.25 seconds.
* Volume up press and Volume down press: the assigned commands run in text fields and on sliders, where the volume keys move the cursor or adjust the slider. A press without a command still moves the cursor or adjusts the slider.
* Speak position: also says where the cursor is in the text of the focused item, such as "Character 120 of 800, paragraph 2 of 5, 15%", and how far the view around the item is scrolled, such as "Scroll position 40%".
* After emoji names: replaces Speak "emoji" after emoji names, with Nothing, Say "emoji" and Play a sound, which plays the Emoji sound after emoji. Go to Settings > Verbosity > After emoji names. The earlier setting carries over.
* Speak only focus moved by the user while an assistant or call plays audio: also applies while an assistant window, such as Gemini, is shown. The screen-on and unlock readouts are left out then, and speech stops when an assistant window opens.
* Typing by voice: after text is typed or deleted by voice, SandFly says the typed text instead of the whole text field.
* Dictate: recognizes the language of the keyboard, such as the language chosen in Gboard, when it differs from the language of the phone.
* Speak dictated text: on by default. Go to Settings > Typing > Dictation.
* Clipboard history: keeps texts of up to 200,000 characters each, and 1,000,000 characters in all, up from 10,000 characters each.
* App shortcuts: from Android 12, with Shizuku started through adb or wireless debugging, or with root, the App shortcuts command category also lists the shortcuts that apps add while they run, such as recent conversations, and shortcuts that only the home screen app may open.
* Command lists: apps are named Open and the name of the app, such as "Open Gmail", to tell them apart from commands of the same name. Voice commands still open apps by the name of the app alone.
* Send feedback: the item for a new report is New feedback. Go to Settings > Help & feedback > Send feedback.
* APIs for other apps: the speech, braille display and direct touch APIs use names under io.github.aaron_gh.sandfly. The earlier names are deprecated and still work. When a debuggable app uses an earlier name, SandFly posts a notification that lists the replacements. The direct touch service has the io.github.aaron_gh.sandfly.keeps_keyboard_region meta-data. The references are in Settings > Help & feedback > User guide, Appendixes E, F and G.
* Problem reports include logs of Focus after Back, Macro Actions conditions, the Gboard popup of alternative characters, Enter presses, what plays and records, and the last announcements, notifications, toasts and window names that apps sent. Text in password fields is logged as the number of characters only.
* User guide: in Polish. Go to Settings > Help & feedback > User guide.
* Indonesian: the app and the user guide use one term for each concept, and the Indonesian user guide covers the same content as the English guide.
* Chinese (Simplified): gesture names use one term for each kind of tap and swipe, such as 双击 and 上滑. The earlier tap phrases still work as voice commands.

## Fixes

* SandFly did not treat the Zinwa Q25 as a keyboard phone when the firmware named Xelex as the manufacturer, so Alt was the SandFly key and the Keyboard phone keymap was not chosen.
* Choosing a keymap could freeze SandFly settings for more than 5 seconds.
* On the Zinwa Q25 lock screen, letter keys and digit keys typed nothing in the PIN entry, and a digit key did not open the PIN pad.
* After the phone was locked in an app with a script that passes every key, such as Keyhub, keys on the lock screen went to Android instead of SandFly when the screen turned on.
* While touch was locked, Activate with a tap, the touch and hold of script rules, and macro touches did nothing.
* After Choose a symbol typed a symbol, the next Space opened the Android character picker instead of typing a space.
* SandFly could run out of memory and stop when the speech of keyboard keys kept failing to synthesize.
* With Time between one-finger taps longer than 0.3 seconds, one-finger double taps did not work, and quick single taps focused nothing.
* During fast typing, explore by touch could turn off and on.
* With Handle two-finger swipes in SandFly on, a two-finger swipe up on the lock screen and the home screen always sent a swipe up, so the command assigned to it, such as Read from top in VoiceOver gestures, did not run.
* On phones that report the built-in buttons as a keyboard, choosing gestures the first time opened the keyboard tutorial instead of the gestures tutorial.
* Keyboards that come with an accessibility service, such as Advanced Braille Keyboard, could lose touches to SandFly gestures while Tap to activate navigation bar buttons was on, in direct touch apps, or when the keyboard was drawn in an accessibility overlay.
* Emoji sent without variation selector 16, such as the ❤ reaction in Telegram, were not named.
* A braille display moved back to the start of the focused item after a device message or a change in the content.
* Editing commands, such as Clear text, did nothing in items that apps mark editable but that are not standard text fields.
* On realme and OPPO phones, SandFly spoke the image descriptions of com.oplus.viewtalk, such as "Image may contain…", in announcements, toasts, notifications and window changes.
* With Hold keys down after the touch and hold delay on, touch exploration did not reach the popup of alternative characters in Gboard 18.8 and later.
* The notification list showed only the title of notifications without body text.
* When a script placed an action first, items without actions, such as text inside a list row, did not list the actions of the row.
* Scroll commands that could not scroll, such as at the end of a list, played no sound.
* In contracted braille, cells typed inside or at the end of a word already typed were translated without the rest of the word, which could produce the wrong text.
* Switch to next granularity and Switch to previous granularity on the braille keyboard could step through the reading controls without end, until Android closed SandFly as not responding.
* In Chrome, Characters and Words did not move through web elements named with aria-label, such as product cards in Google search results, and a swipe moved to the next element.
* A web item outside the visible part of the page could be focused off screen, and was not moved through by character or word.
* Scroll commands run while no item had focus, such as right after another scroll, were slow in large apps such as Gmail.
* With Battery level on, a level reached or an interval ended while the screen was off was never said.
* Auto stop after silence could stop recording during speech on phones with quiet microphone input, and with Rambler live text.
* Screen recognition (SandFly Pro) did not recognize a new screen in the app when the keyboard or System UI reported a change at the same time, or when the screen finished loading after a tap on a recognized item.
* After Back in SandFly settings, focus did not return to the item that opened the screen.
* After Click in the Actions reading control opened a folder, Back moved focus to the first item, such as the Back button, instead of the folder.
* A setting found with Search in SandFly settings was not always focused when the screen with the setting opened.
