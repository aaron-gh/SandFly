## New

* Remove this device (SandFly Pro): removes this device from your SandFly Pro code, so that you can activate the code on another device. Go to Settings > SandFly Pro, and choose Remove this device. SandFly Pro turns off on this device. To buy SandFly Pro or enter a code, go to Settings > SandFly Pro.
* AI actions reading control: runs Describe screen, Speak screen description, Ask about screen, Describe image, Speak image description, Ask about image, and with SandFly Pro, Start or stop AI agent task. Swipe up or down to choose an action, then double-tap to run it. The reading control is in Settings > Reading controls by default. To choose the actions in the reading control, go to Settings > Reading controls > AI actions to include. It needs Android 11 or later.
* Speak and Ask commands: Speak screen description and Speak image description speak the description instead of showing it in a window. Ask about screen and Ask about image take a screenshot of the screen or the focused image, then record your question with dictation, and SandFly speaks the answer. Recording stops after a pause in speech, or when you use the command again. The commands are in Settings > Commands > Image and screen descriptions.
* Report AI response: opens a problem report with the text of the last AI response, for a response that is offensive or wrong. Add what is wrong, and send the report. The command is in Settings > Commands > Image and screen descriptions.
* VoiceOver gestures: you can use the gestures of VoiceOver on iPhone. Go to Settings > Gestures > Gesture layout, and choose VoiceOver gestures. Each layout keeps your changes to the gestures of that layout. In the VoiceOver layout, two-finger swipes run SandFly commands; to turn this on or off in either layout, use Handle two-finger swipes in SandFly. When SandFly starts for the first time, you choose the layout. The new gestures Tap with 4 fingers on top half and Tap with 4 fingers on bottom half are in Settings > Gestures, and the new Speak position command says the position of the focused item in a list or table.
* Back tap: you can run commands with a double or triple tap on the back of the device. Go to Settings > Gestures, and under Back tap, choose Double tap on back or Triple tap on back. To change how firm a tap must be, use Back tap sensitivity.
* Swipes along the edges: you can slide one finger along an edge of the screen: up or down the left or right edge, or left or right along the top or bottom edge. Start at the very edge, and keep your finger near it. To assign commands, go to Settings > Gestures, and under Edges, choose a gesture such as Swipe up along the left edge. These gestures do nothing by default.
* Scroll with one finger along an edge: a slide along an edge moves the content of the app with your finger, as two fingers do. Go to Settings > Gestures, and under Edges, turn on Scroll with one finger along the left edge, right edge, top edge or bottom edge.
* Edge menus: the left and right edges can each be a menu of up to 10 commands. Go to Settings > Gestures > Left edge menu or Right edge menu, turn on Use the left edge as a menu or Use the right edge as a menu, and choose Add command. To use the menu, start at the very edge and slide up or down along it. SandFly speaks each command as your finger reaches it, and lifting your finger runs the command. To cancel, move your finger in from the edge, and lift it.
* Edge width: sets how far from an edge of the screen an edge gesture or an edge menu can start, from 0.5 to 2 cm. Go to Settings > Gestures > Edge width.
* Volume key presses: you can assign commands to double presses, triple presses and long presses of the volume keys. Go to Settings > Volume keys, and choose Volume up double press, Volume up triple press, Volume up long press, or one of the volume down presses. A key with no assigned press changes the volume as before. The presses run no command while the screen is off, during a call, while your finger is on the screen, or during continuous reading. Pressing both volume keys together still runs the accessibility shortcut.
* Hold to cancel: with Lift to activate, holding your finger on an item for a set time makes lifting it activate nothing. Go to Settings > Touch > Hold to cancel, and choose 1, 2, 3 or 5 seconds.
* Tap to activate: a quick tap activates the item under your finger without focusing it first. Within 3 seconds after focus moves, a tap waits for a double tap as before. Go to Settings > Touch, and turn on Tap to activate. This setting needs Android 13 or later.
* Search: Search, at the top of the main settings screen, finds settings and commands on every settings screen. Choosing a result opens the screen of the setting, with the setting focused. Search is also at the top of Settings > Gestures, where it finds a gesture by name or by the command that it runs, of Settings > Keyboard shortcuts, and of the list of commands for a gesture.
* Speech equalizer: you can change the sound of speech with a 12-band equalizer. Go to Settings > Speech > Equalizer, and choose a Preset or move the slider of each band, from 32 Hz to 16 kHz. Each voice profile has its own Equalizer, on the screen of the profile. To turn the equalizer off without changing the bands, turn off Use equalizer. The Equalizer reading control turns the equalizer on or off, and a reading control for each band, such as Equalizer 1 kHz, changes the gain of the band; to add them, go to Settings > Reading controls. The equalizer needs Low-latency audio, in Settings > Audio output.
* Keep speech on the phone while casting: keeps SandFly speech and sounds on the phone during screen casting and mirroring. Screen recordings then have no SandFly audio. Go to Settings > Audio output, and turn on Keep speech on the phone while casting. This setting needs Android 10 or later.
* Announce full charge: SandFly says "Battery fully charged" once when the battery is full while the charger is connected. Go to Settings > Verbosity, and turn on Announce full charge.
* Voice for time announcements: you can choose the voice that says the time at a set interval: the notification voice, SandFly's voice or a voice profile. Go to Settings > Notifications > Summary and time > Voice.
* Use voice profile commands: once you have a voice profile, Settings > Commands > Voice profiles has a Use voice profile command for SandFly default and for each profile, for gestures, edge menus and keyboard shortcuts.
* Speaking while the device is locked: an app that supports it can speak through SandFly while the device is locked, once you allow this separately. The first time, SandFly posts a notification; Allow in it needs the device unlocked. To change this later, go to Settings > Speech > Let other apps speak, and use the switch named after the app, such as "Tasker while the device is locked".
* Links, phone numbers and codes in text: the Actions menu and the Actions reading control list the web addresses, email addresses, phone numbers and codes of 4 to 8 digits in the text of the focused item, also when the app doesn't make them links, such as a one-time code in a message. Choose one to open it, call it or copy it. To choose which kinds are listed, go to Settings > Reading controls > Actions found in text, or Settings > SandFly menu > Actions found in text.
* App shortcuts: commands that open the shortcuts that a long press on an app icon on the home screen shows, such as a new message. To add a shortcut from an app such as MacroDroid, go to Settings > Gestures > App shortcuts, and choose Add app shortcut. The commands are in Settings > Commands > App shortcuts, for gestures, edge menus and keyboard shortcuts.
* Clear text: removes all text from the focused edit box. The command is in Settings > Commands > Text editing.
* Turn color inversion on or off: turns Android color inversion on or off. It needs Shizuku; without Shizuku, the Android accessibility settings open. The command is in Settings > Commands > System actions.
* Turn SandFly keyboard input on or off: while SandFly keyboard input is off, every key of a physical keyboard goes to Android and the app in front, except the keys of this command. The keys are SandFly key + Shift + K, or SandFly key + Shift + Semicolon in the VoiceOver and Orca keymaps. The command is in Settings > Commands > Activation.
* Turn off SandFly: turns SandFly off after you confirm, and gives touch handling back to Android, so that the next screen reader, such as TalkBack, gets your touches. Choose Turn off SandFly in the SandFly menu, or assign the command in Settings > Commands > SandFly.
* Braille display keys: you can change the keys of each braille display command. Go to Settings > Braille > Braille display > Braille display commands, choose a command, choose Change keys, and press the new keys on the display: one key, or Space with dots. Reset restores the default keys of a command, and Reset all keys does this for every command. SandFly commands lists the commands that gestures run, such as Read from top, so that you can give them display keys.
* Braille dictionary: changes text on a braille display, such as a symbol into a word, in all apps or in one app. Its entries are separate from the speech dictionary. Go to Settings > Braille > Braille dictionary, and choose Add a replacement.
* Hide the on-screen keyboard: keeps other on-screen keyboards hidden while a braille display is connected. Go to Settings > Braille > Braille display, and under General, turn on Hide the on-screen keyboard.
* Emoji search: on the braille keyboard, hold dots 4, 5 and 6 and swipe right. Type words of the emoji name in uncontracted braille, and SandFly says the first emoji that matches and how many match. To move between them, swipe down or up with one finger. To insert the emoji, swipe right with two fingers.
* Update dot positions with one hand, then the other: on the braille keyboard, hold three fingers of one hand on the screen, then put down three fingers of the other hand, and keep all six still for 0.65 seconds. To turn this off, go to Settings > Braille > Braille keyboard, and turn off Update dot positions with one hand, then the other.
* Model file: you can use a whisper.cpp model file of your own for dictation, such as a model for one language. Go to Settings > Typing > Dictation, and under Whisper models, choose Model file.
* Correction prompt: you can change the instructions that the AI service gets for correcting dictation. Go to Settings > Typing > Dictation > Correction prompt.
* Go to quoted message: in Telegram, the actions of a message that replies to another include Go to quoted message, which shows the quoted message. To read reply quotes as separate items instead, go to Settings > Navigation, and under Apps, turn off Read Telegram reply quotes with the message.
* Show the focus indicator: to hide the border around the focused item, go to Settings > Display > Focus indicator, and turn off Show the focus indicator.
* Scripts: scripts can now use edge gestures, pinch, spread, two-finger scrub and swipe and hold, change the title and summary of a script setting, and send every key to the app.

## Changes

* Default settings: the default Focus delay is 0.15 seconds, Speak text formatting is off by default, and the default for Window names is Always, cut off by the first item.
* Lift to activate: sliding your finger off the screen, or lifting it at the edge of the screen, activates nothing, and the edge sound plays when your finger reaches the edge. While Lift to activate is on, the bottom 3.5 mm of the screen can't be explored.
* Stopping speech: when touch exploration reaches an item, SandFly stops the speech that started after your finger touched the screen. With Stop speech when touching the screen off, focusing an item by touch stops all speech, including notifications and window names.
* Dictate, play or pause media replaces Play or pause media, start or end voice input, and is the default for a two-finger double tap. It starts Dictate when a text field is being edited, stops a dictation that is recording, and otherwise plays or pauses media. Start or end voice input is removed; gestures and keys assigned to it run Dictate.
* Web pages: swiping is faster in apps with web pages inside other web pages, such as Uber Eats, and SandFly speaks each item as soon as the page moves focus. After you scroll a web page with two fingers, focus moves to an item in view, so that the next swipe continues from there.
* Refresh, and Scroll back or Scroll up at the top of a list, now need Android 13 or later to pull the list down.
* Clipboard history: when you open Clipboard history from an edit box, each entry has Paste.
* Voice profiles: SandFly no longer keeps the engines of all voice profiles started. To keep the engine of the previous voice profile started, go to Settings > Speech > Voice profiles, and turn on Keep the engine of the previous voice profile started. To keep the engine of one profile started, turn on Keep the engine started on the screen of the profile.
* Accessibility volume: while Volume control for speech is not Accessibility volume, the accessibility volume follows the media volume, so voice typing sounds of the keyboard play at the media volume.
* Dictation with Whisper is faster: Whisper skips recordings without speech, and gets no silence before and after the speech.
* Write an app's name to open it and Type an app's name in braille to open it: an app opens only when you open it, also when it is the only match, and SandFly says "No apps" when nothing matches. In Write an app's name to open it, a two-finger swipe right switches between the language of the device and English.
* Braille keyboard swipes count up to about 35 degrees off the horizontal or vertical, instead of 27.
* Vibration strength goes up to 200%, which makes short vibrations stronger and longer. Go to Settings > Sound and vibration > Vibration strength.
* Speak element IDs says the element ID of every unlabelled item that needs a label, not only of images.
* Sound themes can replace each circle menu sound with a file of its own.
* In Settings > Reading controls and Settings > SandFly menu, the options of an item, such as AI actions to include, follow the item, and show only while the item is in the list.
* SandFly has its own icon, a sandfly with S and F in braille in the eyes, for the app, the accessibility service and the SandFly screens.

## Fixes

* After SandFly turned off, the next screen reader, such as TalkBack, could get no touches while another accessibility feature, such as magnification, was on.
* When TalkBack turned off right after SandFly turned on, as with an app that switches screen readers, edge gestures, two-finger rotation and swipe and hold stopped working.
* On Gboard, key names and typing came later than in 0.3.0, split tap didn't type the touched key, and a finger that left the keyboard couldn't explore the rest of the screen without lifting.
* Opening a page, such as a restaurant menu in Uber Eats, held up gestures and speech for over 2 seconds. Automatic descriptions on large web pages held them up for longer.
* On Xiaomi HyperOS, in apps such as Security, swipes played the edge sound or moved to Back instead of moving through the screen.
* In apps such as Pachli and Elten, swiping back through posts taller than the screen moved focus out of the list.
* Scroll left and Scroll right scrolled a vertical list on the page to the end before a pager turned the page.
* While a web page scrolled a strip of tabs sideways, the strip played its own scroll sound.
* EuroBraille Esys displays disconnected right after connecting. On EuroBraille displays, Space chords, the cursor routing keys and Dot 8 now run the same commands as on other displays, and the joysticks move by character and word and activate.
* SandFly stopped at the first touch in Type an app's name in braille to open it.
* The time announcements and the daily summary of notifications could be late while the device was idle. SandFly now says them on time whenever Alarms and reminders is allowed for SandFly, and the Exact times setting is removed.
* A voice profile could use another voice of the same engine after the engine started, and the screen of a profile showed no languages or voices while the engine loaded them.
* Notifications read with a voice profile whose voice is missing were said only after 8 seconds.
* The name of a key or text that is one symbol, such as a period, was spoken in the voice of the language that the symbol was marked as, instead of the voice for the SandFly language.
* SandFly said "Action not supported" when an app ran an action but reported that it failed.
* The reading control switched to Actions on selectable text, such as web page text, for the editing options of the text.
* In custom keymaps, shortcuts with Ctrl or Alt, such as Ctrl + Enter for Submit text, didn't work in a text field being edited.
* Pressing Enter to save a keyboard shortcut also pressed Clear shortcut when the keys were already in use.
* The progress tone kept playing when a request to the AI service failed before it was sent.
