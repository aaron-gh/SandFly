## New

* Screen recognition (SandFly Pro): finds buttons, text and other elements that apps and games draw without making them accessible, and shows them as items that you can swipe to and double-tap. To recognize the current screen, open the SandFly menu and choose Recognize screen. To recognize every screen of an app, open the app, open the SandFly menu, and choose Turn on screen recognition. To see the apps with automatic recognition, go to Settings > SandFly Pro > Automatic screen recognition. To buy SandFly Pro or enter a code, go to Settings > SandFly Pro.
* Keymaps: you can choose a keymap for a physical keyboard: SandFly, VoiceOver, NVDA, JAWS or Orca. The VoiceOver, NVDA, JAWS and Orca keymaps use the shortcuts of those screen readers. Go to Settings > Keyboard shortcuts > Choose a keymap. To make your own keymap from the current one, choose New keymap. If you used the Default keymap, it is kept as a keymap of your own.
* SandFly key for each keymap: each keymap has its own SandFly key, the key that you hold down for SandFly shortcuts. Go to Settings > Keyboard shortcuts > Choose SandFly key. The list shows only the keys that keep every shortcut of the keymap: Meta, Alt, Ctrl + Alt, Ctrl + Meta, Insert or Numpad Insert.
* Keyboard shortcuts in apps: you can assign different commands to keyboard shortcuts in one app. Go to Settings > Keyboard shortcuts > Keyboard shortcuts in apps, and choose the app.
* Browse mode shortcut: you can assign any shortcut to Turn browse mode on or off, including pressing the SandFly key twice. Go to Settings > Keyboard shortcuts.
* Pass keys without the SandFly key to this app: sends keys that SandFly uses without the SandFly key, such as the arrow keys, to the current app, for apps and games that use them. To stop, use the command again.
* Keyboard tutorial: the keyboard tutorial is now interactive, like the touch tutorial. You press each shortcut, and SandFly checks it. Go to Settings > Help & feedback > SandFly tutorial > Keyboard tutorial.
* Arrow keys and Tab: in the NVDA, JAWS and Orca keymaps, you use the keys as on a computer, without the SandFly key. Down Arrow and Up Arrow move to the next or previous item, Left Arrow and Right Arrow move by character, and Ctrl with Left Arrow or Right Arrow moves by word. Tab and Shift + Tab move to the next or previous item that can take focus. Enter activates the item, and Escape goes back. Meta + D, or Ctrl + Alt + D in the Orca keymap, goes to the Home screen. In a text field, these keys type and move the cursor as usual.
* Object navigation: in the NVDA, JAWS and Orca keymaps, you can move to the object that contains the current item, to the first object inside it, and to the next or previous object. The commands are in Settings > Commands > Object navigation.
* Review cursor: you can read the screen by line, word and character without moving SandFly focus, and click or long-click at the cursor. The commands are in Settings > Commands > Screen review.
* Recognize text on screen: reads all the text on the screen, including text in pictures. Use the review cursor to move through the text, and press Escape to close it. The command is in Settings > Commands > Screen review.
* Gesture profiles: you can switch between sets of commands for your gestures. A gesture profile comes before gestures in apps and scripts. Go to Settings > Gestures > Gesture profiles, and choose Add gesture profile. To change the profile, use Choose gesture profile, Next gesture profile or Previous gesture profile, the Gesture profile reading control, or the SandFly menu.
* Gestures in apps: you can assign different commands to gestures in one app. Go to Settings > Gestures > Gestures in apps, and choose the app. The list includes every installed app, with Search at the top.
* Swipe and hold: each one-finger swipe also has a swipe and hold: swipe, keep your finger still at the end, then lift it. You can assign a command to each one in Settings > Gestures. To change how long you hold, go to Settings > Gestures > Hold time after a swipe.
* Tap to activate navigation bar buttons: to activate the buttons in the navigation bar with one tap, go to Settings > Touch, and turn on Tap to activate navigation bar buttons. This setting is separate from Lift to activate.
* Speech dictionary: you can change how text is spoken, in all apps or in one app. Go to Settings > Speech > Speech dictionary, and choose Add a replacement.
* Let other apps speak: apps can ask to speak text with SandFly speech. To allow or stop an app, go to Settings > Speech > Let other apps speak. The current keyboard app can speak without asking, and can turn off SandFly keyboard echo for text that you type with it.
* Reset accessibility audio after calls: after a call, Android can keep sending speech to the earpiece. To move SandFly speech back, go to Settings > Audio output, and turn on Reset accessibility audio after calls. This setting needs Shizuku.
* Type an app's name in braille to open it: turns the screen into a braille keyboard. Type the first letters of the app name in uncontracted braille. SandFly announces the first app that matches, and opens it when only one app matches. To move between the apps that match, swipe down or up with one finger. To open the app, swipe up with two fingers. To close, swipe down with two fingers.
* Japanese braille: you can now use Japanese braille, based on the rules of libkuraji. The Japanese dictionary downloads the first time you use it. To choose Japanese, go to Languages in the braille keyboard settings or the braille display settings.
* Submit text: sends the text in the current text field, with the send button next to it or the action key on the keyboard, such as Send or Search.
* Clipboard history: lists the text that you copied, newest first, to copy again or remove. Clipboard history is a command in Settings > Commands > Text editing, and you can add it to the SandFly menu. While Shizuku allows SandFly, text that you copy in other apps is added too; to stop this, turn off Add copies from other apps in Clipboard history. Passwords and other text marked sensitive are not added. Add to clipboard adds text after the text already on the clipboard.
* Turn magnification on or off: turns Android screen magnification on or off.
* Rewind and Fast forward: move back or forward in the media that is playing.
* Accessibility shortcuts: these commands run the accessibility shortcut of another feature, such as Reading mode. They need Shizuku. The commands are in Settings > Commands > Accessibility shortcuts.
* Activate with a tap: taps the screen where the current item is, for items that do nothing when SandFly activates them.
* Stop reading the notification: stops the notification that SandFly is reading, also in the voice of a voice profile.
* Scripts: scripts can now touch the screen, press keys, run SandFly commands, open windows with rows, headings and sliders, recognize text, read device information, and speak during calls, recording and dictation. Before you install a script, SandFly shows where it comes from and whether SandFly signed it.
* SandFly as the wallpaper: some phones stop background apps to save battery. To keep SandFly running, go to Settings > Display, and choose Use SandFly as the wallpaper. To choose the picture, use Wallpaper image.

## Changes

* Sounds: SandFly has new sounds, and every sound has a vibration. Focus sounds and list chimes are lower, reading control sounds are quieter, and a gesture that does nothing plays two short falling notes. You can still replace the sounds with a sound theme.
* The click sound plays as soon as you activate an item, and the new state of a switch is announced as soon as the click is done.
* Activating an item stops speech, also when Stop speech when touching the screen is off.
* Typed letters are announced sooner.
* Swipes no longer skip items when a list scrolls.
* While a physical keyboard is connected, the braille keyboard is hidden, like other on-screen keyboards. It stays on screen while a braille display is connected, or when Use on-screen keyboard is on in Android settings.
* The braille keyboard plays SandFly typing sounds, and Say "Submitting text" is now a braille keyboard setting.
* Vertical navigation stays in the column of the current item, such as from key to key on a keypad.
* Gestures and dictionary entries for an app stay in use while the keyboard or a system window is open over the app, and on launchers, such as the Xiaomi launcher, that report no change when the Home screen opens.
* SandFly waits for the HID host of a paired Bluetooth braille display to connect before it connects to the display.
* The Meta key is named Meta instead of Action.

## Fixes

* When you touch and hold a key in Gboard, SandFly announces the selected alternative character. Before, SandFly announced the popup of alternative characters as a new keyboard, and the name of the selected character was cut off.
* The One UI "N more notifications" placeholder was read as a notification.
* Focus that an app removed during a call, or while another app recorded, came back only after the call or recording ended.
