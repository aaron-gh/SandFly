## New

* Keymaps for physical keyboards: SandFly, VoiceOver, NVDA, JAWS and Orca, under Settings > Keyboard shortcuts > Choose a keymap. Each keymap has its own SandFly key, chosen from the keys that keep all its shortcuts: Meta, Alt, Ctrl + Alt, Ctrl + Meta, Insert or Numpad Insert. New keymap makes a keymap of your own, and the Default keymap is kept as one.
* In the NVDA, JAWS and Orca keymaps, the arrow keys and Tab move without the SandFly key, as on a computer. They add object navigation, a review cursor, and Recognize text on screen, which reads text that apps draw without exposing it. Escape goes back, and Meta + D (Ctrl + Alt + D in Orca) goes to the home screen. Pass keys without the SandFly key to this app gives apps that use single keys their keys.
* Browse mode is one shortcut that can be changed, including to the SandFly key pressed twice.
* Gesture profiles, under Settings > Gestures > Gesture profiles, give gestures other commands, ahead of gestures in apps and scripts. Next gesture profile and Previous gesture profile switch between them.
* Gestures in apps and Keyboard shortcuts in apps give gestures and keys other commands in chosen apps. Choosing an app lists every installed app, with Search first.
* A swipe and hold for each one-finger swipe: swipe, rest the finger, then lift. Each can have its own command. Settings > Gestures > Hold time after a swipe sets how long the finger rests.
* Speech dictionary, under Settings > Speech, says chosen text differently in all speech.
* Clipboard history and Add to clipboard. While Shizuku allows SandFly, copies made in other apps go into the history too, unless Add copies from other apps is off on the history's screen. Clips marked sensitive, such as passwords, are left out.
* Let other apps speak, under Settings > Speech, lets apps speak through SandFly. The current keyboard can speak through SandFly without asking and turn off SandFly's echo of its typing.
* Type an app's name in braille to open it: a command that turns the screen into a braille keyboard for the start of an app's name.
* Japanese braille, from libkuraji's rules, with a dictionary downloaded when it is first needed.
* The keyboard tutorial is interactive, like the touch tutorial.
* Script API version 2: touch, keys, SandFly commands, windows with rows, headings and sliders, text recognition, device information and more. Scripts speak during calls, recording and dictation. Before installing, SandFly shows where a script came from and whether SandFly signed it.
* Commands: Submit text sends the focused edit box's text with the app's send button or the keyboard's action. Turn magnification on or off starts and stops Android's magnification. Rewind and Fast forward control playing media. Accessibility shortcuts run other features' accessibility shortcuts, such as Reading mode, through Shizuku. Activate with a tap taps the focused item where it is.
* A gesture or key can stop the notification being read.
* Settings > Display > Use SandFly as the wallpaper keeps SandFly running on phones whose battery savers stop it.
* Settings > Audio output > Reset accessibility audio after calls moves SandFly's speech off the earpiece when Android leaves it there after a call. It needs Shizuku.
* Settings > Touch > Tap to activate navigation bar buttons, apart from Lift to activate.
* SandFly Pro screen recognition finds buttons, text and other elements that apps and games draw without exposing them, and shows them as items to swipe to and double tap. Recognize screen, in the SandFly menu, recognizes the current screen. Turn on screen recognition in (the app), in the SandFly menu, or Settings > SandFly Pro > Automatic screen recognition recognizes every screen of an app.

## Changes

* SandFly's sounds are its own, synthesized, and every sound has a vibration. Focus sounds and list chimes are lower, the reading control sounds quieter, and a gesture that did nothing plays two quick falling notes.
* The click sound plays at once, and a switch's new state is said as soon as the click is done.
* Activating an item stops speech, also with Stop speech when touching the screen off.
* Typed letters are said sooner.
* Swipes no longer skip items when a list scrolls.
* The braille keyboard is hidden while a physical keyboard is connected, unless Android's Use on-screen keyboard is on. Its typing sounds are SandFly's own, and Say "Submitting text" is a braille keyboard setting.
* Vertical navigation stays in the focused item's column, such as from key to key on a keypad.
* Gestures and dictionary entries for an app stay in use while the keyboard or a system window is over it, and follow the app in front on launchers that don't report it, such as Xiaomi's.
* A paired Bluetooth braille display that connects by HID is waited for until its HID host connects.
* The Meta key is named Meta instead of Action.

## Fixes

* One UI's "N more notifications" placeholder was read as a notification.
* Focus lost in an app during a call or a recording in another app came back only after it ended.
