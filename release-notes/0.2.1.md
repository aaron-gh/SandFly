## Changes

* Edge gestures play a sound at each stage: a low tick when a finger touches the edge, a higher tone at the first click and two rising tones at the second click. Each has a switch in Individual sounds and vibrations, and sound themes can replace them.

## Fixes

* After a phone call on One UI, speech moved between the earpiece and the speaker, or stayed in the earpiece.
* On phones that keep the call audio mode after a call, every utterance waited 3 seconds.
* With Phone speaker chosen, SandFly turned on the speakerphone or changed the call's audio device when it started during a call.
* A speech engine that was slow to answer lost low-latency audio until SandFly restarted, also when it was busy with another app's speech.
* Ask by voice, voice commands and the SandFly Pro agent's prompt kept recording until the gesture was repeated when Auto stop after silence was off. They now stop 2 seconds after speech pauses.
* Voice commands didn't match with Google on-device recognition, Whisper, Rambler or AI correction, which add punctuation.
* Voice commands stopped working after one was started during dictation. When recognition ended by itself, speech stayed muted.
* With Rambler chosen, the audio of a dictated password was sent to Gemini.
* With Quiet while recording on, notifications were still read while the camera, a voice recorder or a call in an app recorded.
* A notification dismissed while waiting to be read was still read, and dismissing the one being read dropped the others waiting.
* Bluetooth headphone conditions for notification reading never matched.
* Notifications on the locked phone ignored While the screen is touched and scripts that change notification speech. The same message posted twice while locked was not read.
* Automatic translation, Translate last spoken phrase and scripts cancelled each other's AI service translations. Turning automatic translation off dropped notifications being translated.
* Choosing an AI service's model from its list didn't put the service in use.
* Describe image and Describe screen failed offline with the on-device model.
* Live recognition stopped SandFly when its AI service was no longer set up, such as after a SandFly Pro code was revoked. An empty Describe screen answer stopped SandFly.
* Importing a settings file with a value of the wrong type stopped SandFly at every start. Export settings, Import settings and the direct touch backup held up speech while a cloud storage app downloaded or uploaded.
* A script update with a bad manifest stopped SandFly at every start. An update could bring back a script that was removed or a permission that was turned off.
* A script stuck in one call stopped every script. It is now turned off after 20 seconds and the others keep running.
* A script could use unlimited memory, and apps with unusual values or empty items could stop SandFly through scripts.
* Sound themes and scripts that another app sent as a link were downloaded before SandFly asked. A link now asks Download or Cancel first.
* An app installed under the name of an approved braille display app was used without asking. The approval screen was empty after the screen rotated.
* After scrolling back to the top of a list, touches went to the app for about a second.
* Two-finger touches didn't pass through to the app when no rotation gesture was assigned.
* Direct touch stayed off after a text field in a game lost focus. The circle menu and the handwriting keyboard removed direct touch's area.
* Typing in the granular editing dialogs and the tutorial's typing practice stalled and lost keys.
* "Write an app's name" waited for every app's name to load before the prompt. The handwriting keyboard repeated its instruction every 20 seconds.
* The tutorial didn't read the lesson again when reopened, stopped after continuous reading ended in the background, and restarted when a keyboard was connected.
* The keyboard tutorial placed the Action key above the left Shift key and offered Action + Alt + T, which does nothing.
* Quick settings tiles and script commands that use Shizuku stopped working after Shizuku's service restarted.
* Appear installed from Google Play and updates through Shizuku installed SandFly in the work profile, private space and other users.
* Without Modify system settings, each step of the quick settings brightness slider opened that settings screen again.
* An update download stopped when SandFly was turned off.
* A Verbosity reading control chosen before 0.2.0 stayed current and did nothing.
* An app whose question about speaking was dismissed was never asked again until SandFly restarted.
* The update download replaced the notification to uninstall the old app.
* In Report a problem, a reply that arrived while its report was open stayed unread and played a notification. A title with a line break was refused.
