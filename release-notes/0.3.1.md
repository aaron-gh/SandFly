## Changes

* SandFly Pro activation: Export settings no longer includes the SandFly Pro activation. When one activation is in use on more than one device, SandFly Pro is turned off on all of them, and SandFly says why. To use SandFly Pro again, go to Settings > SandFly Pro and enter your code. If you no longer have the code, ask for it in Settings > Help & feedback > Send feedback.

## Fixes

* With SandFly Pro chosen as the AI service but not active, image descriptions and other AI features used Gemini or reported a problem with the Gemini API key. SandFly now says that SandFly Pro is not active and where to enter a code.
* On some phones, including the Galaxy Z Fold7 and Xiaomi phones with Android 16, SandFly said nothing in the Gboard popup of alternative characters, and lifting your finger typed a character that SandFly had not read. SandFly now reads the selected character as you move your finger in the popup, and lifting your finger types it.
