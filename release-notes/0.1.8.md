## New

* Write an app's name to open it, a gesture action in System actions, opens a writing area on the whole screen. Write the start of an app's name: SandFly says the first app whose name, or a later word of it, starts with those letters, and how many apps do, and opens the app when only one matches. Swipe up with two fingers for the next app, down to open it, left to delete the last letter, and tap with two fingers to hear the app again.
* Whisper models offers FUTO's models, Tiny, Base and Small, in English and multilingual, from 44 to 264 MB. They process only the audio you spoke instead of 30 seconds, so short dictation is much faster than with the other models.
* Braille display settings > Advanced settings > Cursor blinks every has Never, which keeps the cursor dots steady.
* Braille display settings > Advanced settings > Show cursor on non-editable items, on by default. Turned off, the cursor is shown only in text fields.

## Changes

* Whisper decodes each dictation once. When a result failed Whisper's checks, it was decoded again up to five more times.
* Touch and activation > Reduce delay > Time between one-finger taps also offers 0.3, 0.4 and 0.5 seconds, for slower double taps.
* Check for updates shows the release notes for a stable release, instead of every change since your build.
* Send feedback with the log attached sends a much longer log, the logs of SandFly's other processes, the log of the screen reader's last process when the report is sent after SandFly was turned off, the log from when SandFly was last turned off, and how SandFly's last processes ended.
* With a small on-device model, such as FastVLM 0.5B, Describe screen describes the screenshot in plain sentences, told the app's name and given the screen's text to copy names from. It repeated the example answer in its instructions, or described things that weren't on the screen.
* Describe screen and Describe image hide the focus indicator in their screenshot, so it isn't described as part of the screen.
* The machine translations of all languages were corrected.

## Fixes

* Whisper dictation took over a minute for a few seconds of speech: its speech recognition was built without the compiler's optimization.
* Whisper on the GPU crashed SandFly while loading the model on Snapdragon phones such as the Galaxy Z Fold7. If the GPU driver crashes while loading, the next dictation uses the CPU.
* Whisper on the GPU prepared the GPU for about 20 seconds before every dictation; it now does this once.
* A double tap whose second tap came more than about 0.25 seconds after the first tap started was taken as two single taps, even when the second tap came within Time between one-finger taps of the first one ending.
* Notifications of an app with its own voice profile were silent, though Notification history listed them as read, once the profile's speech engine had been stopped by Android or the engine itself, until the app's voice was changed.
* A notification in SandFly's voice that arrived while speech was off or SandFly was paused held back the notifications for other voices until SandFly restarted.
* Turning on TalkBack after SandFly left TalkBack silent until SandFly was force stopped, as SandFly's notification listener kept it running.
* Sounds of a custom sound theme sometimes played late during fast navigation.
* After an audio underrun on some phones, every sound played a few milliseconds later than needed.
* In Telegram's chat list, swiping to a chat could read the chat that row showed before.
* On-device AI on the NPU of Snapdragon phones failed at every request.
* Some service descriptions and the welcome text still said TalkBack.
