## New

* Live recognition describes what the rear camera sees. Open it from SandFly settings or the Live recognition reading control. Its modes turn on and off separately: Scenes, Text, Point and Speak (the text above or below a finger in the camera view), People detection and Door detection. In the reading control, swipe up or down to choose a mode and double-tap to turn it on or off. On the screen, a two-finger tap stops speech and speaks the next result, and a two-finger scrub closes it. Text is recognized on the phone. The other modes use what Describe image uses: the on-device model, the AI service in use, or Gemini Live, after a confirmation. Descriptions can be wrong and aren't for moving around safely.

## Changes

* Notification history shows what happened to each notification: waiting, read, read in SandFly's voice because its voice profile's engine failed, or not read because speech was silenced, it was stopped before it started, or too many were waiting. A notification showed as read as soon as SandFly decided to read it.
* While speech is turned off, focus and action sounds and vibrations still play.
* Questions about an image or the screen asked by voice are recognized by Google on-device recognition first, as dictation is, and by the phone's other speech recognition when one fails.
* Add voice profile opens an empty name field. OK with the field empty uses the suggested name.
* SandFly's process ends after SandFly is turned off, unless a SandFly screen, the SandFly keyboard, a download or a feedback report is still in use.

## Fixes

* Ask by voice said "No speech recognized" and "No answer available" at once on phones whose speech recognition doesn't serve other apps.
* Turning on TalkBack after SandFly still left TalkBack silent on some phones until SandFly was force stopped.
* Speech stayed in the earpiece after a call during which Bluetooth earbuds were connected.
* With Audio output device set to Phone speaker, speech could go to the earpiece.
* The list of models of OpenAI and other AI services failed with "Illegal argument exception" when the pasted API key had a line break or invisible character in it.
* A braille display whose pairing failed or was rejected stayed on connecting, and choosing it again did nothing until SandFly restarted. A failed pairing now shows the connection failure.
* Notifications of an app with its own voice profile could stay silent while Notification history listed them as read, when a notification whose speech ended without starting, such as during a call, held back the others.
* A notification whose voice profile's engine failed or never started speaking wasn't read. It is now read in SandFly's voice.
* Notifications in another voice kept speaking over dictation.
* "Spoken feedback off" and "Spoken feedback on" were not spoken while other audio played.
* In an empty text field with a hint, such as a rule's name, the first character or word typed was read twice, even with keyboard echo off.
* API key, address, model and script text fields offered no Paste until the field was touched.
* Typing in notification rule fields could stall SandFly.
* A voice profile name typed with a Gboard-based keyboard, such as PixelBoard, came out in capitals.
* Another app opening an unknown SandFly settings screen crashed SandFly.
