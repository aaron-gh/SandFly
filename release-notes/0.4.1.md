## Changes

* Addresses and numbers: the Actions menu and the Actions reading control no longer list the web addresses, email addresses, phone numbers and codes of 4 to 8 digits in the text of the focused item. They are now in the Addresses and numbers reading control and the Addresses and numbers item in the SandFly menu, which are off by default. To add the item, go to Settings > Reading controls or Settings > SandFly menu. In the reading control, swipe up or down to choose an address or number, then double-tap to open it, call it or copy it. To choose which kinds are listed, go to Addresses and numbers to include, on the same screen.

## Fixes

* With Samsung Keyboard, SandFly did not read the keys while you moved your finger over the keyboard, in any typing method or language. SandFly now reads each key.
* On web pages, the reading control switched to Actions on text with links or addresses, so a swipe up or down ran an editing option instead of moving to the next heading. The reading control now stays on that text.
* After the reading control switched to Actions automatically on an item with no actions, a swipe up or down reported no actions. The swipe now uses the previous reading control.
* On Samsung phones with One UI, the SandFly notification list showed an empty item for the Now Bar media notification. SandFly no longer lists that notification.
