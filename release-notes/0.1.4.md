## New

* Advanced settings > Use SandFly's quick settings makes the Quick settings action open a panel of SandFly's instead of the system's quick settings. It is a grid of switches, sliders and buttons, in one to four columns, read with each item's row and column. Advanced settings > Quick settings panel adds, removes and moves its items: Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, mobile data, hotspot, airplane mode, Do Not Disturb, sound mode, flashlight and its brightness, location, auto-rotate, battery saver, dark theme, NFC, brightness, adaptive brightness, each volume, data saver, Night Light, Extra dim, color inversion, color correction, microphone and camera access, screen timeout, font size, media, media output, next alarm, battery, Settings, the power menu, apps' tiles and the system's own tiles. Most system controls need Shizuku. The System quick settings gesture action opens the system's quick settings.
* Settings > More > Export settings saves all of SandFly's settings to a file, and Import settings replaces them with those of such a file, to set up another phone the same way. API keys are not in the file. Custom labels, scripts and sound themes are not in it either.
* Advanced settings > Appear installed from Google Play makes Android record Google Play as SandFly's installer, for apps, such as some banking apps, that turn off accessibility services installed from anywhere else. It needs Shizuku. Updates keep the recorded installer, and SandFly puts it back if an update drops it.
* SandFly has an app icon that opens its settings, also while SandFly is off.
* Gesture actions: Lock screen and Power menu in System actions; Increase media volume and Decrease media volume in Other; Scroll to top and Scroll to bottom, which scroll the list to its start or end and focus its first or last item.
* The braille keyboard gesture Switch to next braille table, holding dots 4-5-6 and swiping down by default, goes to the next braille keyboard language. Contracted braille is turned on or off as it was when that language was last used, and speech changes to a voice profile in the language of the table if there is one.
* Touch and activation > Volume keys move the cursor: while a text field has focus, volume up moves the cursor back a character and volume down moves it forward.
* Touch and activation > Stop speech when touching the screen. When off, touching the screen no longer stops speech until touch focuses an item.
* Reading controls > Links and Controls only in web content leaves those two reading controls out of the list outside web content.
* Automatic descriptions > Other AI service sends Describe image and Describe screen to a service with an OpenAI-compatible API, such as OpenRouter, Groq, Mistral, Ollama Cloud or Claude, instead of Gemini.
* Sound themes > Install a theme > From a source installs themes from a repository on GitHub, GitLab, Codeberg, Gitea or Bitbucket, as Script sources does for scripts.
* Script sources lists SandFly's own scripts, github.com/aaron-gh/sandfly-scripts, until it is removed.
* SandFly's notification list shortens a notification's text to about 160 characters. Its Show all action reads and shows the whole text.
* SandFly's strings are translated into Vietnamese and Simplified Chinese.

## Changes

* Touch exploration no longer reads the home screen page, such as "Home screen 1 of 2", when the finger lands between icons. Swiping still reaches it.
* An item that is named by the text inside it, such as a button whose label is a separate view, follows Order of item details. Before, its type was always read first.
* On a watch, an update downloads over Wi-Fi when it is available, and its progress shows in a notification. A notification opens the install prompt when Android doesn't open it.
* The notification list stops reading notifications while it is off, names a group by the notification shown newest, and shows a group without a summary one notification at a time.

## Fixes

* The time, battery, status, window title and other announcements that a gesture or key asks for are spoken while another app, such as TeamTalk, records from the microphone, and during calls.
* The quick settings panel's last row showed nothing when it wasn't full, such as with 13 items in two columns.
* The Brightness slider follows the phone's own brightness range, which on some phones is wider than 1 to 255, so its percentage matches the system's.
* After the apps list and App info, home screen icons in some launchers, such as Hyperion, could not be reached by touch or swipe, only the dock. SandFly now clears Android's cached copy of the home screen when it opens again, on Android 13 and later.
* Calls answered on the phone could move to a paired watch, because SandFly reset the call audio route when Audio output was System default.
* Web elements that Chrome names, such as posts on X, no longer read their parts again after the name.
* Swipes no longer wait for an app that stops responding.
