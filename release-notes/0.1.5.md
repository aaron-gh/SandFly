## New

* Notifications is a settings category of its own, with full control of how notifications are read: a speech format with placeholders, speech rate, pitch and volume, a separate voice from a voice profile, language detection, per-app settings, words that must or must not appear, replacements, kinds of notifications to skip, when to read (screen off, locked, headphones, media playing, calls, Do Not Disturb, quiet hours, while the screen is touched), rules with conditions and actions, repeats, ways to stop reading (shake, wave, both volume keys, dismissing), a summary and spoken time, a history of the last 100 notifications, a quick settings tile, gesture actions, and broadcasts for automation apps.
* Report a problem has New report, an Inbox and Drafts. Sent reports show their status and the developer's replies, and you can reply from SandFly. A reply or a change of status posts a notification with sound. What you type in a report is kept as a draft until you send it.
* Automatic descriptions has an AI services section: AI service chooses Gemini, Claude, OpenAI (ChatGPT), Mistral, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama Cloud, Other service or None, and each service has its own settings screen with its API key and model. Gemini settings has Use Gemini and Gemini model.
* Edges in gesture settings has ten more gestures: a long swipe down from the top left and top right, and a swipe and a long swipe in from the top half and the bottom half of the left and right edges. They do nothing by default. Only stages that have an action click, and a swipe from an edge whose gestures do nothing is an ordinary swipe.
* The Vertical navigation reading control moves to the item below or above, past the other items of the same row, such as the buttons beside a video or a file.
* Text-to-speech > Speech volume chooses the accessibility, media, ring or call volume for speech and sounds, and the Speech volume reading control switches between them. The ring volume keeps speech on the phone when media is cast to a TV.
* Reading controls > Change reading control automatically can be turned off, so that a reading control you chose, such as Headings, isn't replaced by Actions when focus moves onto an item with actions.
* Import settings also reads a settings file exported from Backtalk.
* Scripts are updated automatically from where they were installed, once a day, when nothing you agreed to changes. Other updates are listed under Updates in Scripts.

## Changes

* Telegram reply quotes are read with their message, after the time, with the whole quoted text when the quoted message is loaded, and are no longer focused on their own.
* Describe image and Describe screen ask Gemini to think as little as it can, and ask Gemini Flash-Lite when Gemini Flash is busy or takes more than 15 seconds.
* Pressing Ctrl alone on a keyboard pauses speech without the app acting on Ctrl, so YouTube no longer shows its keyboard shortcuts.
* When choosing a gesture's action, the categories of actions are collapsed, except the one with the action in use.

## Fixes

* Touching a card in recent apps said nothing in launchers such as OnePlus's.
* Touching an empty part of the lock screen said "Lock screen".
* A slowly starting swipe could focus the item under the finger instead of moving.
* Describe image and Describe screen said "Something went wrong" when on-device AI was chosen but its model wasn't installed, and said nothing when Gemini was busy.
* Notifications were not read while media played, while the screen was touched, while Do Not Disturb allowed them, or when Read when screen off was on, and some apps' notifications were read twice.
* One UI's "MediaOngoingActivity" notification was read even with media players skipped.
