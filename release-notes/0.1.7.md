## New

* Dictation. The Dictate gesture action types what you say into the focused text field, with any keyboard. The gesture starts recording, and recording goes on through pauses until you perform the gesture again. Speech is silenced while recording.
* Settings > Dictation chooses the speech recognition: Automatic, Google on-device speech recognition, Rambler (Gemini API), Whisper or Android speech recognition. Automatic uses the first of these that can be used.
* Google on-device speech recognition runs on the phone with the offline language packs of Speech Recognition & Synthesis from Google, the engine Gboard's voice typing uses, and adds punctuation. When the phone's language isn't downloaded, Dictation settings has a row that opens its download screen.
* Rambler (Gemini API) sends the recording to Gemini 3.5 Transcribe, the model behind Gboard's Rambler, with the Gemini API key from Gemini settings. It removes filler words, keeps only your correction when you correct yourself, and formats numbers, dates and lists. Rambler live transcription sends the audio while you speak, so the text is ready soon after you stop. With a free Gemini API key, Google may use the audio and text to improve its products.
* Whisper recognizes speech inside SandFly, with nothing sent anywhere. Whisper models downloads Tiny, Base, Small or Large v3 Turbo, from 32 to 574 MB.
* Correct dictation with AI cleans up the text of Google on-device speech recognition, Whisper and Android speech recognition as Rambler does, with the AI service for dictation: the one for automatic descriptions, another AI service that is set up, or on-device AI, which keeps all of dictation on the phone. A password field's text is never sent.
* Handwriting keyboard: an input method that types what you write on the screen with one finger, with two-finger and three-finger swipes for space, delete, new line, other readings and switching keyboards, a tutorial, and its own keyboard echo setting. Typing and braille > Wait after writing sets how long it waits for the next stroke. Handwriting is recognized on the phone; the model for your language, about 20 MB, is downloaded the first time, after asking.
* Write the letter of a reading control, a gesture action, chooses a reading control by a letter written on the screen. Reading controls > Handwriting letters sets each control's letter, mostly NVDA's quick navigation keys, such as K for Links and A for Landmarks.
* Granular editing, in a text field's Editing actions and as a gesture action, opens the text in a screen of its own split into characters, words, sentences, lines or paragraphs, to edit, insert, delete, select, find, replace, undo and redo, then save it back to the field.
* Turn browse mode on or off is a gesture action and can have a second keyboard shortcut. Navigation > Remember browse mode in each app keeps browse mode as you last left it in each app.
* Refresh, a gesture action and keyboard shortcut, scrolls the list to its top and pulls it down, which refreshes it in most apps. Scroll back and Scroll up at the top of a list pull it down too.
* Sound and vibration > Vibration strength, on phones that can vary it, scales every vibration.
* Gestures > Edges > Distance to first click and Distance from first to second click set how far an edge gesture slides to each click.
* Notifications > Apps > an app > Voice reads that app's notifications with a voice profile of its own, and rules can read a notification with a voice profile.
* EuroBraille b.note, b.note 2, b.book and b.book 2 braille displays work over USB, with their keys named.
* On-device AI runs on the NPU of Google Tensor G5 and G6 (Gemma 4 E2B) and Snapdragon 8 Elite and 8 Elite Gen 5 (FastVLM 0.5B), with the model built for that NPU. Whisper runs on the GPU of Snapdragon phones. The NPU and GPU options appear only on phones that have them, and their libraries are downloaded or loaded only there.
* Send feedback with the log attached includes SandFly's last crash, from any of its processes.

## Changes

* SandFly is now fully translated into all of its languages. Many languages had TalkBack's translations only, so SandFly's own settings and messages were in English.
* Report a problem is called Send feedback and is also in the SandFly menu.
* The notification settings are on a Notifications screen, in Feedback after Sound and vibration, instead of filling the main settings screen.
* Sound feedback volume is a slider from 5 to 100 percent in steps of 5.
* On-device AI's Use the GPU is now Processor, which is shown only on phones whose GPU or NPU on-device AI can use.
* AI services no longer have Use switches. AI service lists the services that are set up, and None. A service you set up is used when no other one is, and when the service in use loses its key or model, the next one that is set up takes over. With no service in use, descriptions and questions say so.
* Vertical navigation stays in the list: it scrolls the list a page when no row above or below is on screen, instead of moving to the next item, such as the app's tabs, and it passes over rows that scroll sideways.
* Next container and Previous container stop at toolbars, tab bars, search bars and panes, such as YouTube's top bar. A bar inside a bar is one stop, and Previous container lands at the start of the container.
* SandFly does less work for every event, touch and swipe. Debug tracing and log text, which every build made, are now made only when Developer settings > Log output level is Debug or Verbose, and the log then also shows how each gesture was recognized.

## Fixes

* Describe image, Describe screen and questions went to Gemini even when another AI service or on-device AI was chosen.
* The question buttons said Ask Gemini whatever the AI service; they are Ask by voice and Type a question.
* The API key error named Other AI service instead of the service's own settings.
* The capital letter pitch or sound wasn't used when exploring keyboard keys.
* Vertical navigation going up could land on a part of a row, such as a setting's summary, or on a row partly hidden under a toolbar.
* Scroll to top and Scroll to bottom in a list that doesn't scroll focused the top or bottom of the screen, such as Navigate up.
* After the Shizuku server stopped or restarted, switches that need Shizuku did nothing until SandFly restarted.
* The Send feedback keyboard shortcut did nothing.
* Adding a notification rule didn't open its screen, and the rule was listed only after going back.
* A voice profile used as the notification voice read at another rate than in SandFly when Android's default speech rate or pitch wasn't 100 percent, and without the profile's voice, volume and phrases.
* System UI notifications were read again when only their details changed.
* The voice command "type" typed nothing in apps such as Google Contacts.
* After a reboot, switching to your speech engine could hold up SandFly for up to 14 seconds on a slow phone. It now switches as soon as the phone is unlocked.
