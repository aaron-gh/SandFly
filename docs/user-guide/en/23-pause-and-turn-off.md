## Pause and turn off SandFly {#pause}

You can stop SandFly for a while in three ways:

- Stop speech only. SandFly still handles gestures. See [](#pause-speech).
- Pause SandFly. The device works as if no screen reader is on, and SandFly resumes at once. See [](#pause-pause).
- Turn off SandFly. See [](#pause-turn-off).

The settings for pausing and turning off are in **Pause and turn off**.

### Stop speech without pausing SandFly {#pause-speech}

- **Pause or resume speech**, a two-finger tap by default, stops speech and continues it from where it stopped.
- **Turn speech on or off**, a three-finger double-tap by default, turns speech off until you use the command again. SandFly still plays sounds and vibrations and handles gestures.

See [](#speech).

### Pause SandFly {#pause-pause}

While SandFly is paused, it doesn't speak, play sounds or vibrate, and doesn't handle gestures or explore by touch. Touches go to the apps as if no screen reader is on. SandFly stays on, so it resumes at once.

To pause SandFly, do one of the following:

- Choose **Pause SandFly** in the SandFly menu. It is in the menu by default.
- Assign a gesture to the **Pause SandFly** command, or a key combination to the **Pause or resume SandFly** keyboard shortcut, and use it. See [](#commands) and [](#keyboards).

The **Pause SandFly?** dialog opens, with how to resume. Choose **Pause**. To pause without the dialog next time, clear **Always show this warning** before you choose **Pause**.

SandFly says "SandFly paused", removes the focus highlight from the screen, and shows the notification **SandFly is paused**. Live recognition stops.

While SandFly is paused:

- The volume keys change the volume.
- The keys of a physical keyboard go to the app.
- If SandFly restarts, such as after the device restarts, it starts unpaused.

Pause SandFly isn't available on watches and TVs.

### Resume SandFly {#pause-resume}

To resume SandFly, do one of the following:

- Tap the notification **SandFly is paused**. It is in the silent section of the notification shade.
- Press volume down three times quickly.
- Press the **Pause or resume SandFly** keyboard shortcut, if one is assigned.
- Lock and wake the phone, or turn the screen off and on, depending on the **Resume SandFly** setting.

SandFly says "SandFly resumed".

**Pause and turn off** > **Resume SandFly** chooses when SandFly also resumes by itself:

- **When the lock screen shows**: SandFly resumes when the screen turns on and the lock screen shows. This is the default.
- **When the screen turns on**: SandFly resumes each time the screen turns on, also without the lock screen.
- **Only from the notification or a shortcut**: SandFly resumes only from the notification, three presses of volume down, or the keyboard shortcut.

The **Pause SandFly?** dialog names the ways to resume that this setting allows. **Resume SandFly** isn't shown on watches and TVs.

### Turn off SandFly {#pause-turn-off}

To turn off SandFly from SandFly:

1. Choose **Turn off SandFly** in the SandFly menu, or use the **Turn off SandFly** command with a gesture. The menu item is in the menu by default.
2. In the **Turn off SandFly?** dialog, choose **Turn off**. **Keep on** closes the dialog.

You can also turn off SandFly with the accessibility shortcut, a Quick Settings tile, or the switch in the Android accessibility settings. See [](#getting-started-shortcut).

When SandFly turns off in any of these ways, it gives touch handling back to Android, so that the next screen reader that you turn on gets touches and gestures. After SandFly is off, its process ends within a few seconds, unless a SandFly screen is open, the SandFly keyboard is in use, or an update download or feedback report is in progress.

On a watch, the SandFly menu item is named **Turn off**.

### Instructions for turning off SandFly {#pause-exit-banner}

In some cases after SandFly turns on, such as when the pages about new features are closed before the last page, SandFly shows a banner at the top of the screen: "SandFly on. Hold 2 fingers on screen for 5 seconds to turn off." While the banner is shown, touch and hold the screen with two fingers for 5 seconds to turn SandFly off.

The banner goes away when you open the SandFly settings or open the SandFly menu, and SandFly then stops showing it. To stop showing it yourself, turn off **Pause and turn off** > **Show instructions for turning off SandFly**. The setting is on by default. It isn't shown on watches and TVs, which don't show the banner.
